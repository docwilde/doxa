"DOXA" and the DOXA logo are trademarks of Fabian Wilde. The AGPL-3.0
licence grants rights to the code; it does not grant rights to the name
or the mark, the same separation AGPL/Apache-licensed projects commonly
draw between copyright and branding.

## What a fork may do

Say, accurately, that it is derived from or based on DOXA. Link back to
this repository. Keep the copyright and licence notices the AGPL already
requires.

## What a fork may not do

Call itself DOXA, or use the DOXA name or logo in a way that implies it
is DOXA, is endorsed by DOXA, or is the official project. Use the name or
mark in its own project name, package name, or branding without
permission.

## Asking

If your fork needs to use the name beyond fair, accurate reference to its
origin, open an issue at
[github.com/docwilde/doxa/issues](https://github.com/docwilde/doxa/issues)
or reach out via [github.com/docwilde](https://github.com/docwilde).
